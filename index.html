📐 The Weissman Score Standard

Weissman Metric Formula:
$$W = \alpha \cdot \frac{r}{\overline{r}} \cdot \frac{\log_{10}{\overline{T}}}{\log_{10}{T}}$$

Where $r$ and $T$ are the compression ratio and compression time of the target algorithm, while $\overline{r}$ and $\overline{T}$ represent standard baseline metrics (Gzip).
