Compression Benchmark Suite

        
Comparative performance analysis evaluating compression ratio ($r$), execution time ($T$), and Weissman Score ($W$).

      


      

        
        

          

            
📐 Weissman Score Metric

          

          

            

              
Weissman Standard Equation:

              

                $$W = \alpha \cdot \frac{r}{\overline{r}} \cdot \frac{\log_{10}{\overline{T}}}{\log_{10}{T}}$$
              

              

                Where $r$ and $T$ represent compression ratio and execution time against standard baseline standards ($\overline{r}, \overline{T}$).
